1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 24 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-309.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 29.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 8 May 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 7 May 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Contract Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 29.

6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 3 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 24 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 1.0% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 10% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £50,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11.1  This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter.
11.2  Each party acknowledges it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.
11.3  Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud.

12.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
12.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

16.1  Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 21 days of notice.

16.3  Termination does not affect rights accrued before the date of termination.

17.2  Supplier shall maintain adequate procedures to prevent bribery by persons associated with it.
17.3  Breach of this clause entitles the Purchaser to terminate immediately by written notice.

18.2  A waiver is effective only if given in writing and signed by the waiving party.
18.3  A waiver of one breach is not a waiver of any later breach.

19.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
19.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

20.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
20.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.

21.2  Documents shall be in English and in the format agreed with the Purchaser.
21.3  Delivery is not complete until the data book has been accepted by the Purchaser.

22.3  Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

25.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 5 years after delivery.
25.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

26.2  If the event continues for more than 120 days, either party may terminate the affected Purchase Order by written notice.
