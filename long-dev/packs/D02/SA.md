3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 15 April 2026 (the "Effective Date").

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Contract Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 27.

8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 12 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

13.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
13.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

14.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

19.3  Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud.

20.1  A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right.
20.2  A waiver is effective only if given in writing and signed by the waiving party.
20.3  A waiver of one breach is not a waiver of any later breach.

21.1  The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by escalation to senior management for 30 days.
21.2  If the dispute is not resolved, either party may refer it to mediation before starting proceedings.
21.3  Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

22.2  Documents shall be in English and in the format agreed with the Purchaser.

24.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
24.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

25.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
25.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.
