SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-3233 dated 2 January 2026
Between Wexcombe Gas Systems Ltd (the Purchaser) and Orrin Fabrication Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 18 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-769.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 18.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 2 January 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 2 January 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Total Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 18.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 14 January 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 20 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 3 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 18 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 0.5% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 10% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £2,500,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY
11.1  Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
11.2  These obligations continue for 5 years after the end of this Agreement.
11.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

12. AUDIT
12.1  The Purchaser may audit Supplier's quality records relating to the Goods on 30 days' notice during normal working hours.
12.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 5 years after delivery.
12.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

13. ASSIGNMENT
13.1  Neither party may assign or transfer its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.
13.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
13.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.

14. INSURANCE
14.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
14.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
14.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
15.1  The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by escalation to senior management for 30 days.
15.2  If the dispute is not resolved, either party may refer it to mediation before starting proceedings.
15.3  Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

16. PACKING AND SHIPPING
16.1  Supplier shall pack the Goods to protect them against damage and corrosion during transport and storage.
16.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
16.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

17. SUBCONTRACTING
17.1  Supplier shall not subcontract any critical manufacturing step without the Purchaser's written approval.
17.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
17.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

18. VARIATIONS
18.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
18.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

19. GOVERNING LAW
19.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
19.2  The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
