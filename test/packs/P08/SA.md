SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-2502 dated 9 March 2026
Between Tarn Process Engineering Ltd (the Purchaser) and Halden Valve Works Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 24 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-183.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 20.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 9 March 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 8 March 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Contract Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 20.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 8 February 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 16 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 5 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 24 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 0.5% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 10% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £1,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. NOTICES
11.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
11.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
11.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY
12.1  Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
12.2  These obligations continue for 2 years after the end of this Agreement.
12.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

13. DATA PROTECTION
13.1  Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in relation to personal data processed under this Agreement.
13.2  Supplier shall process personal data only on the Purchaser's documented instructions.
13.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

14. PACKING AND SHIPPING
14.1  Supplier shall pack the Goods to protect them against damage and corrosion during transport and storage.
14.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
14.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

15. TERMINATION
15.1  Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 21 days of notice.
15.2  The Purchaser may terminate for convenience on 120 days' written notice, paying for Goods delivered and work in progress.
15.3  Termination does not affect rights accrued before the date of termination.

16. AUDIT
16.1  The Purchaser may audit Supplier's quality records relating to the Goods on 21 days' notice during normal working hours.
16.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 2 years after delivery.
16.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

17. SUBCONTRACTING
17.1  Supplier shall not subcontract any critical manufacturing step without the Purchaser's written approval.
17.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
17.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

18. INSURANCE
18.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £20,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
18.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £20,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
18.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

19. WAIVER
19.1  A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right.
19.2  A waiver is effective only if given in writing and signed by the waiving party.
19.3  A waiver of one breach is not a waiver of any later breach.

20. VARIATIONS
20.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
20.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

21. GOVERNING LAW
21.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
21.2  The courts of Singapore have exclusive jurisdiction.
