SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-9435 dated 23 February 2026
Between Kestrel Offshore Services Ltd (the Purchaser) and Halden Valve Works Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 12 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-835.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 20.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 23 February 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 23 February 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Total Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 20.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 18 March 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 20 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 5 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 12 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 0.5% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 10% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £50,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY
11.1  Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
11.2  These obligations continue for 2 years after the end of this Agreement.
11.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

12. QUALITY SYSTEM
12.1  Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 or equivalent throughout the Term.
12.2  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser within 21 days if its certification is suspended or withdrawn.
12.3  Supplier shall implement the corrective actions the Purchaser reasonably requires after an audit.

13. SEVERABILITY
13.1  If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement continues in effect.
13.2  The parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid replacement that achieves the original intent.
13.3  This clause does not apply where removing the provision defeats the main purpose of this Agreement.

14. NOTICES
14.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
14.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
14.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

15. DATA PROTECTION
15.1  Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in relation to personal data processed under this Agreement.
15.2  Supplier shall process personal data only on the Purchaser's documented instructions.
15.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

16. ASSIGNMENT
16.1  Neither party may assign or transfer its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.
16.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
16.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.

17. SUBCONTRACTING
17.1  Supplier shall not subcontract any critical manufacturing step without the Purchaser's written approval.
17.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
17.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

18. PACKING AND SHIPPING
18.1  Supplier shall pack the Goods to protect them against damage and corrosion during transport and storage.
18.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
18.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

19. INSURANCE
19.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
19.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
19.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

20. VARIATIONS
20.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
20.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

21. GOVERNING LAW
21.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
21.2  The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
