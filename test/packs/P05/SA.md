SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-4090 dated 26 February 2026
Between Brindle Marine & Energy Ltd (the Purchaser) and Halden Valve Works Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 18 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-354.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 19.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 26 February 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 26 February 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Total Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 19.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 18 January 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 12 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 5 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 18 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 1.0% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 5% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £50,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. NOTICES
11.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
11.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
11.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
12.1  This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter.
12.2  Each party acknowledges it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.
12.3  Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY
13.1  Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
13.2  These obligations continue for 5 years after the end of this Agreement.
13.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

14. QUALITY SYSTEM
14.1  Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 or equivalent throughout the Term.
14.2  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser within 30 days if its certification is suspended or withdrawn.
14.3  Supplier shall implement the corrective actions the Purchaser reasonably requires after an audit.

15. TERMINATION
15.1  Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of notice.
15.2  The Purchaser may terminate for convenience on 60 days' written notice, paying for Goods delivered and work in progress.
15.3  Termination does not affect rights accrued before the date of termination.

16. AUDIT
16.1  The Purchaser may audit Supplier's quality records relating to the Goods on 30 days' notice during normal working hours.
16.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 5 years after delivery.
16.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

17. FORCE MAJEURE
17.1  Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party within 30 days.
17.2  If the event continues for more than 60 days, either party may terminate the affected Purchase Order by written notice.
17.3  Shortage of labour or materials caused by the affected party's own supply chain choices is not force majeure.

18. INSURANCE
18.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
18.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £10,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
18.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

19. VARIATIONS
19.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
19.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

20. GOVERNING LAW
20.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
20.2  The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
