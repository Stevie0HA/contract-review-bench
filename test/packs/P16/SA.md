SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-7145 dated 4 April 2026
Between Kestrel Offshore Services Ltd (the Purchaser) and Halden Valve Works Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 18 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-460.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 18.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 4 April 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 3 April 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Contract Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 18.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 10 February 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 12 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 3 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 18 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 1.0% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 10% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £50,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. NOTICES
11.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
11.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
11.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

12. SUBCONTRACTING
12.1  Supplier shall not subcontract any critical manufacturing step without the Purchaser's written approval.
12.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
12.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

13. FORCE MAJEURE
13.1  Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party within 21 days.
13.2  If the event continues for more than 60 days, either party may terminate the affected Purchase Order by written notice.
13.3  Shortage of labour or materials caused by the affected party's own supply chain choices is not force majeure.

14. INSURANCE
14.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
14.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
14.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

15. AUDIT
15.1  The Purchaser may audit Supplier's quality records relating to the Goods on 21 days' notice during normal working hours.
15.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 5 years after delivery.
15.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

16. DATA PROTECTION
16.1  Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in relation to personal data processed under this Agreement.
16.2  Supplier shall process personal data only on the Purchaser's documented instructions.
16.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

17. PACKING AND SHIPPING
17.1  Supplier shall pack the Goods to protect them against damage and corrosion during transport and storage.
17.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
17.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

18. VARIATIONS
18.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
18.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

19. GOVERNING LAW
19.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
19.2  The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
