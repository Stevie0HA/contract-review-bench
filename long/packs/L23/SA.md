SA.H  SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. SA-2026-5546 dated 28 February 2026
Between Northgate Energy Projects Ltd (the Purchaser) and Halden Valve Works Ltd (the Supplier).

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1  "Contract Price" means the total price payable for the Goods as set out in Schedule 1.
1.2  "Warranty Period" means 12 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.
1.4  "Purchase Order" means an order for Goods placed by the Purchaser under this Agreement.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-290.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 29.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 28 February 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 28 February 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Contract Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 29.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 21 December 2026 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 12 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 60 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 3 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 12 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 0.5% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 5% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £2,500,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. PACKING AND SHIPPING
11.1  Supplier shall pack the Goods to protect them against damage and corrosion during transport and storage.
11.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
11.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

12. WAIVER
12.1  A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right.
12.2  A waiver is effective only if given in writing and signed by the waiving party.
12.3  A waiver of one breach is not a waiver of any later breach.

13. TERMINATION
13.1  Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 21 days of notice.
13.2  The Purchaser may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice, paying for Goods delivered and work in progress.
13.3  Termination does not affect rights accrued before the date of termination.

14. DATA PROTECTION
14.1  Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law in relation to personal data processed under this Agreement.
14.2  Supplier shall process personal data only on the Purchaser's documented instructions.
14.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

15. SUBCONTRACTING
15.1  Supplier shall not subcontract any critical manufacturing step without the Purchaser's written approval.
15.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
15.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

16. ANTI-BRIBERY
16.1  Each party shall comply with all applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws.
16.2  Supplier shall maintain adequate procedures to prevent bribery by persons associated with it.
16.3  Breach of this clause entitles the Purchaser to terminate immediately by written notice.

17. QUALITY SYSTEM
17.1  Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 or equivalent throughout the Term.
17.2  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser within 21 days if its certification is suspended or withdrawn.
17.3  Supplier shall implement the corrective actions the Purchaser reasonably requires after an audit.

18. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
18.1  This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter.
18.2  Each party acknowledges it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.
18.3  Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud.

19. FORCE MAJEURE
19.1  Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party within 21 days.
19.2  If the event continues for more than 90 days, either party may terminate the affected Purchase Order by written notice.
19.3  Shortage of labour or materials caused by the affected party's own supply chain choices is not force majeure.

20. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
20.1  The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by escalation to senior management for 21 days.
20.2  If the dispute is not resolved, either party may refer it to mediation before starting proceedings.
20.3  Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

21. ASSIGNMENT
21.1  Neither party may assign or transfer its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.
21.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
21.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.

22. AUDIT
22.1  The Purchaser may audit Supplier's quality records relating to the Goods on 21 days' notice during normal working hours.
22.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 2 years after delivery.
22.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

23. SEVERABILITY
23.1  If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement continues in effect.
23.2  The parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid replacement that achieves the original intent.
23.3  This clause does not apply where removing the provision defeats the main purpose of this Agreement.

24. CONFIDENTIALITY
24.1  Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
24.2  These obligations continue for 2 years after the end of this Agreement.
24.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

25. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
25.1  Each party retains ownership of the intellectual property it brings to this Agreement.
25.2  Supplier grants the Purchaser a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the manuals and drawings supplied with the Goods.
25.3  Supplier shall indemnify the Purchaser against claims that the Goods infringe third-party rights, subject to prompt notice of the claim.

26. INSURANCE
26.1  Supplier shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for each claim throughout the Term.
26.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
26.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

27. DOCUMENTATION
27.1  Supplier shall supply a data book with each delivery containing material certificates, test reports and inspection release certificates.
27.2  Documents shall be in English and in the format agreed with the Purchaser.
27.3  Delivery is not complete until the data book has been accepted by the Purchaser.

28. NOTICES
28.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
28.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
28.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

29. VARIATIONS
29.1  No variation is effective unless recorded in a written Variation Order signed by both parties.
29.2  A Variation Order shall state the original and revised quantities and the effect on the price.

30. GOVERNING LAW
30.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
30.2  The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
