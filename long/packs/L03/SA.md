1. DEFINITIONS

1.2  "Warranty Period" means 18 months from the date of delivery of the Goods.
1.3  "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule 1 and specified in Schedule 2.

2. SCOPE
2.1  Supplier shall supply the Goods in accordance with Schedule 2 and Specification TS-114.
2.2  The Purchaser may order up to the Maximum Quantity of each item stated in Schedule 1. Orders above the Maximum Quantity require a Variation under clause 29.

3. TERM
3.1  This Agreement takes effect on 4 February 2026 (the "Effective Date").
3.2  This Agreement continues until 4 February 2028 and shall not renew automatically; any extension requires written agreement signed by both parties.

4. PRICE
4.1  The Contract Price is the ceiling value in Schedule 1. Prices are firm and fixed for the Term.
4.2  The Total Price shall not be subject to adjustment except under clause 29.

5. DELIVERY
5.1  Supplier shall deliver the Goods by the Delivery Date stated in each Purchase Order and in any case no later than 31 January 2027 (the "Long-Stop Delivery Date").
5.2  Each Purchase Order shall allow not less than 20 weeks from the date of the Purchase Order to the Delivery Date.

6. PAYMENT
6.1  The Purchaser shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.
6.2  Invoices shall be issued only after the Inspection Release Certificate has been issued under clause 7.

7. INSPECTION
7.1  The Purchaser may inspect the Goods at any stage of manufacture on 3 working days' notice.
7.2  The shell hydrostatic test is a hold point and the final inspection is a witness point, as set out in Schedule 4.
7.3  All test equipment shall hold a valid calibration certificate on the date of the test.

8. WARRANTY
8.1  Supplier warrants the Goods against defects for 18 months from delivery.
8.2  Supplier shall repair or replace defective Goods at its own cost within a reasonable time.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1  If delivery is late, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages at 0.5% of the value of the late Goods for each complete week of delay.
9.2  Liquidated damages shall not exceed 5% of the value of the late Goods.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1  Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to £1,000,000.00, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and fraud.
10.2  Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. INSURANCE

11.2  Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for the duration of the Warranty Period.
11.3  Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on written request.

12.2  Each package shall be marked with the Purchase Order number, item code and gross weight.
12.3  Risk passes to the Purchaser on delivery; title passes on payment.

13.2  The parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid replacement that achieves the original intent.
13.3  This clause does not apply where removing the provision defeats the main purpose of this Agreement.

14.2  Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
14.3  Supplier shall flow down the inspection and records requirements of this Agreement to approved subcontractors.

16.2  Supplier shall process personal data only on the Purchaser's documented instructions.
16.3  Supplier shall notify the Purchaser without undue delay of any personal data breach.

17. WAIVER
17.1  A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right.
17.2  A waiver is effective only if given in writing and signed by the waiving party.
17.3  A waiver of one breach is not a waiver of any later breach.

18. NOTICES
18.1  Notices shall be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party stated in this Agreement.
18.2  A notice delivered by hand is received on delivery and a notice sent by recorded post is received on the second working day after posting.
18.3  Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

19. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
19.1  The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute by escalation to senior management for 21 days.
19.2  If the dispute is not resolved, either party may refer it to mediation before starting proceedings.
19.3  Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

20. TERMINATION
20.1  Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 21 days of notice.
20.2  The Purchaser may terminate for convenience on 60 days' written notice, paying for Goods delivered and work in progress.
20.3  Termination does not affect rights accrued before the date of termination.

21.1  Neither party may assign or transfer its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other.
21.2  Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld for an assignment to a group company.
21.3  The assignor remains responsible for the acts of its assignee.

22.3  Disclosure required by law or by a regulator is permitted if the disclosing party gives prior notice where lawful.

24.2  Supplier shall retain manufacturing and inspection records for 3 years after delivery.
24.3  Each party bears its own costs of an audit unless it reveals a material non-conformance.

25.2  Each party acknowledges it has not relied on any statement not set out in this Agreement.
